Shell - Free Educational Game for Young Children

In this educational game, children discover animals that carry their home on their back. Every time they interact, a friendly turtle, a snail, and a hermit crab appear, emerging from their shells, sparking the curiosity and smiles of the little ones. Designed for babies and toddlers aged 1 to 3, this game helps them relate a simple action to an immediate result, stimulating attention, observation, and coordination. It's a fun and safe way to take the first steps on the computer or mobile device while they meet new animals.
