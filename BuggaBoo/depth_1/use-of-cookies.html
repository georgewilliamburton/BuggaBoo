Cookie Policy

OWLIE BOO uses cookies to enhance your user experience, measure website activity and personalise the advertisements displayed on the site. Ad personalisation is essential for us to be able to continue to offer our site free of charge. Below we give you all the information you need to know exactly what cookies are, what they are used for, what types of cookies we use, for what purpose and how you can set or disable them if you wish.

Cookies: What are they and what are they for?

A cookie is a file that is stored on the user's device in order to learn more about how the user browses the site. The information that all of our users' cookies provide us with allows us to improve our site so that it is better adapted to your tastes and needs. Cookies are fundamental to the development of the Internet, as they allow the development of services more in line with the wishes and needs of users. In no way can cookies damage your computer, but on the contrary they allow us to detect and solve problems.

What types of cookies does OWLIE BOO use?

Third-party cookies:
These are cookies that are sent to your device from a server or computer that is not managed by us. In the case of OWLIE BOO, as in many sites around the world, cookies are managed by Google to collect navigation data for analytical and advertising purposes. To learn more about how Google uses cookies click here.

Session cookies:

These are cookies that are stored in the cookie file of your browser when you enter the website and are automatically deleted when you leave it. They are used to find out how our users use the site, which pages and sections are most visited, which allows us to improve our content.

Analysis cookies:
These are cookies that allow us to know the number of visitors and the type of navigation that different users have on our site. From this statistical data we can develop new services and improve the quality of the site in general.

Advertising cookies:
These are cookies that enable the management of advertising spaces in the most efficient way possible.

Behavioural advertising cookies:
These are cookies that store data over time on the browsing habits of each user, which allows us to offer advertisements that are more in line with the browsing profile.

Social cookies:
Some services may use connectors with various social networks: Facebook and Instagram. By using the social register, you authorise the social network to place cookies. These cookies add the social component to your browsing.
